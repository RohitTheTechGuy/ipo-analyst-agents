Lessons from past analyses. Apply each one the digest supports; skip one whose items are missing. A
lesson is never evidence by itself: cite the digest items it names. To add a lesson, append one line in
the same form (lesson, then "check:" the digest items, then the source in brackets).

1. Everything the call needs is in the RHP. Where a figure isn't printed, the digest has it computed from
   RHP numbers (D: items, V:multiples); use those rather than calling it undisclosed. check: D:*, V:multiples
   [user rule, Oct 2026]
2. Work out every standard multiple from the financials, not only the ones the RHP prints. Prefer EV/EBIT
   for depreciation-heavy businesses (telecom, power, towers), and use post-issue EV. check: V:multiples
   [Jio Platforms CFA note, Oct 2026]
3. Being priced level with the listed leader is not a margin of safety for a long-term buy, especially
   with lower returns than the leader. check: V:multiples, V:read (RoE vs peer median)
   [Jio Platforms CFA note, Oct 2026]
4. A thin free float with a large pre-IPO stake that unlocks after the lock-in means supply hits long-term
   holders months after listing. check: D:free_float_pct, D:unlockable_pct, D:overhang_x_float,
   G:UNLOCK_OVERHANG [Jio Platforms CFA note, Oct 2026]
5. A brand the promoter group owns and can withdraw, or charges a royalty for, is a governance risk; weigh it
   in conviction. check: G:BRAND_LICENSED [Jio Platforms CFA note, Oct 2026]
6. Judge cash generation by free cash flow and the capex trend, not by PAT alone; falling capex intensity
   that turns FCF positive is a real improvement. check: C:free_cash_flow, C:pat_positive_cfo_negative
   [Jio Platforms CFA note, Oct 2026]
7. Judge leverage after the issue proceeds repay debt, not before. check: D:net_debt_to_ebitda,
   D:net_debt_to_ebitda_post [Jio Platforms CFA note, Oct 2026]
8. High related-party share of revenue can be a sales channel (e.g. a group company as sole distributor)
   rather than leakage: read the categories and counterparties before calling it a red flag.
   check: C:rpt_vs_revenue, F:fine_print_legal.related_party [Jio Platforms CFA note, Oct 2026]
